US work authorization and pay types

The terms I explain most often during initial candidate conversations.

W2, C2C and 1099

W2: you are an employee, with taxes withheld and possible benefits. C2C: you contract through your own company, which bills the vendor or client. 1099: you are paid directly as an independent contractor. For C2C and 1099, tax and insurance responsibilities differ based on the arrangement and applicable law.

H-1B, H-4 EAD, OPT, STEM OPT and CPT

H-1B is an employer-sponsored status for specialty occupations, and eligible holders may change employers through the applicable petition process. Certain H-4 spouses may qualify for an EAD. F-1 students may have work authorization through OPT, STEM OPT (if eligible) or CPT subject to program and regulatory requirements.

TN, green card and US citizens

TN status covers certain Canadian and Mexican professionals under USMCA. US citizens and lawful permanent residents generally have unrestricted US employment authorization.

General information only, not legal advice. Confirm individual cases with qualified immigration counsel and current USCIS guidance.
